The photographs in this directory are Poly Haven assets, released under CC0 1.0. They are free, public-domain assets; APEX CLUB does not claim authorship of these photographs.

License: https://polyhaven.com/license and https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The five surface sets contain 1024-pixel color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps. The sky uses a 1K Radiance HDR panorama. All are served locally without a runtime dependency on Poly Haven. `provenance.json` records the official download URL, authors, source hash and local file hash. JPEG maps are recompressed for the web; no game textures or models have been extracted from QQ Speed.

The HDR parser is the official Three.js r179 `RGBELoader.js`, matching the existing bundled renderer. Its source is https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r179/examples/jsm/loaders/RGBELoader.js and its MIT license is retained at `vendor/three/LICENSE`.
