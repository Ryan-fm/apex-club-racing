APEX CLUB is a 3D arcade racer you can play in your browser. Take your kart through sweeping coastal bends, ancient city gates and a neon-lit harbor. Charge a drift, catch the exit, take-off and landing boost windows, and turn a small burst of speed into your next overtake.

No game client to download. The game interface supports English and Simplified Chinese; switch languages in the top-right corner.

## Three tracks, three rhythms

**Bay Circuit**

Accelerate along the coast and across bridges, finding your line through wide, sweeping bends. Sea, islands and a lighthouse set the scene for three laps.

**Jade Citadel**

Pass through city gates and follow ancient walls toward distant mountains. Linked corners, elevation changes and golden ramps bring drifting and jumping together.

![TITAN at the start of the Jade Citadel three-turn practice segment](docs/images/citadel.jpg)

**Neon Harbor**

Race through neon arches, climb to the top of an elevated bridge, then tackle linked hairpins, dockside S-bends and a lower turnaround section. A central ring landmark, bridge cables and harbor cranes shape the midnight skyline. Brake early and plan your drift through the tight turns.

## Your kart, your style

Browse the garage from side to side, rotate the camera around your kart, then choose its paint, livery and exhaust effects.

![TITAN in the APEX CLUB garage, with 3D preview, car selection and paint options](docs/images/garage.jpg)

| Kart | Driving character |
| --- | --- |
| **COMET** | Light and agile, built for quick direction changes |
| **APEX** | Balanced speed and handling for your first race |
| **BOLT** | Focused on top speed; plan corner entries early |
| **SLIDE** | A drift specialist with faster charge buildup |
| **TITAN** | A wide-track armored buggy with oversized tires and exposed suspension |
| **VINTAGE** | Classic round headlights and a quick launch |

Combine gloss, satin or metallic paint with twin stripes or a checkered livery. Finish with jet or pulse exhaust effects. Customization changes appearance while keeping your kart's driving characteristics intact. Your choices are saved in the current browser for your next visit.

## Chase the podium, or score for your team

- **Solo race:** compete against seven AI drivers over three laps.
- **4v4 team race:** you and three AI teammates face four AI opponents, earning team points by finishing position.
- **Practice:** learn steering, braking, drifting and mini boosts in the driving lesson. The current development version also includes a separate three-turn gate challenge.

Time an EMP to disrupt nearby opponents and open a passing opportunity; teammates are protected in team races. Chase your personal best, race your own ghost and earn bronze, silver and gold challenge medals.

*This is a single-player game with AI competitors. Online multiplayer is not currently supported.*

## Your first lap starts here

Choose a kart and track, then click **Launch race**. First-time players can start with the driving lesson.

Auto throttle is on by default; you handle steering and braking. Start with this sequence: **hold Shift and steer to drift → release Shift after charging → press W for a mini boost.** Releasing the drift alone does not fire a boost.

| Default key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **← / →** | Steer left / right |
| **↑ / ↓** | Accelerate / brake; keep holding ↓ at rest to reverse |
| **Shift + direction** | Drift; keep Shift held while countersteering |
| **W** | Trigger an available mini, air or landing boost |
| **Ctrl** | Use nitro |
| **Q** | Fire EMP |
| **H / Esc** | Pause, view controls or recover your kart to the track |
| **R** | Restart the race |

Change bindings in **Settings → Custom hotkeys**. Once the basics feel natural, try chaining countersteer cut boosts, air boosts and landing boosts.

**On your phone:** landscape is recommended. Use the on-screen steering, drift, mini boost, nitro, brake, throttle and EMP buttons. Supported devices can enable tilt steering in the phone controls settings; touch arrows always take priority. Sensor permissions and fullscreen support depend on your device and browser.

**Find your pace:** enable beginner assists, reduce camera motion, or adjust graphics and sound in Settings. If you get stuck, try braking into reverse, or pause and choose to recover your kart to the track.
